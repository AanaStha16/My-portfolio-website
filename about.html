About Me

Hello! I am a passionate vandal currently expecting to graduate in May 2027. During my time at the university, I have immersed myself in a rigorous curriculum spanning data structures, quantum mechanics, electrodynamics, and software engineering. My goal is to leverage this multidisciplinary background to build intelligent, efficient software systems.

Beyond the classroom, I am deeply involved in campus life. I work as a Student Technical Support staff member at the IT Service Desk, helping peers and faculty troubleshoot complex networking and software issues. I also serve as an International Peer Mentor, guiding new students as they navigate their academic and personal transitions.

When I am away from my keyboard or the physics lab, I maintain a strong balance of creative and physical hobbies. I am an avid table tennis player and a former high school singles and doubles champion. I also enjoy writing poetry, participating in debates, and exploring public speaking opportunities.
